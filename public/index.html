HOW IT WORKS · ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE

From checkout idea to verifiable delivery.

Business goal: enable a customer to complete a purchase successfully. Here's how AI can help turn that goal into delivery artefacts that a team can review and verify.

STEP 01 · DISCOVER

Define the outcome

Business idea: A customer can review their cart, provide the required checkout details and place an order.

Human decision: Confirm the intended customer experience, business rules and scope.

STEP 02 · ANALYSE

Challenge assumptions

AI assistance: Identify questions about empty carts, invalid details, payment failures, duplicate submissions and order confirmation.

Human review: Resolve business rules before treating assumptions as requirements.

STEP 03 · SPECIFY

Make it testable

Requirement: The system must create an order only after the required checkout validations and payment outcome are successful.

Acceptance criterion: Given valid checkout details and a successful payment, when the customer places the order, then one order is recorded and a confirmation is displayed.

STEP 04 · VERIFY

Connect tests to evidence

Planned tests: Successful checkout, invalid details, declined payment and repeated submission.

Evidence: Link each requirement to test results and review the actual implementation before claiming it is verified.

Human accountability remains throughout. AI can propose requirements, edge cases and tests. People confirm business rules, approve scope and assess the evidence.

This is an illustrative workflow, not a live checkout implementation. The example's planned tests must not be confused with executed test results.
